I'm Ayumi, a Singapore-based singer-songwriter and university student. My English pop single "I Don't Have" came out on 9 Oct 2026. My next Instagram chapter is "i don't have it figured out": a Singapore girl in Taipei, romanticising the little things while pretending she's completely fine.

My feed today: lo-fi, shot on iPhone, outdoors in daylight. I sing on the grass with headphones, a handheld mic, a laptop and a small red audio interface on a white sheet.
